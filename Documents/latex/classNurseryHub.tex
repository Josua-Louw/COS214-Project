\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Concrete Mediator implementation. 

Maintains registries of plants, staff, and customers. Implements the {\ttfamily notify}, {\ttfamily assign}, and registration operations declared by \doxylink{classNurseryMediator}{Nursery\+Mediator}.
