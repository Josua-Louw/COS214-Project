\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents the seed state in a plant\textquotesingle{}s lifecycle. 

The \doxylink{classSeedState}{Seed\+State} class models the initial phase where the plant is a seed, preparing to germinate. It inherits from \doxylink{classPlantState}{Plant\+State}, part of the plant lifecycle state machine. Care actions in this state, such as watering and fertilizing, are critical for successful germination and progression to the seedling state. \begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
This is the starting point of the plant\textquotesingle{}s lifecycle. 
\end{DoxyNote}

Transitions the plant to the next state in its lifecycle. 

Moves the plant from the seed state to the seedling state if appropriate care has been provided. If care is insufficient, the plant transitions to the dying state. \begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
The next state is typically the \doxylink{classSeedlingState}{Seedling\+State} or, in case of inadequate care, the \doxylink{classDyingState}{Dying\+State}.
