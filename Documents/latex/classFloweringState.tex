\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents the flowering state in a plant\textquotesingle{}s lifecycle. 

The \doxylink{classFloweringState}{Flowering\+State} class models the phase where the plant is actively producing flowers, a critical stage for reproduction. It inherits from \doxylink{classPlantState}{Plant\+State}, part of the plant lifecycle state machine. Care actions, such as watering and fertilizing, are essential to sustain flowering and influence whether the plant returns to a mature state, progresses to senescence, or dies due to insufficient care. \begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
This stage is pivotal for the plant’s reproductive success and subsequent lifecycle transitions. 
\end{DoxyNote}

Transitions the plant to the next state in its lifecycle. 

Moves the plant from the flowering state to either the mature state, the senescence state, or the dying state if care is insufficient. The decision between mature and senescence states depends on the care strategy, which is not implemented in this method. \begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
The next state is typically the \doxylink{classMatureState}{Mature\+State}, \doxylink{classSenescenceState}{Senescence\+State}, or \doxylink{classDyingState}{Dying\+State}, based on care actions and strategy.
