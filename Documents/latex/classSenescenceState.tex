\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents the senescence state in a plant\textquotesingle{}s lifecycle. 

The \doxylink{classSenescenceState}{Senescence\+State} class models the phase where a plant is aging and no longer actively growing. This is the final stage before the plant transitions to a dying state. It inherits from \doxylink{classPlantState}{Plant\+State}, part of the plant lifecycle state machine. \begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
This is the end of the plant\textquotesingle{}s active lifecycle, and most care actions are ineffective. 
\end{DoxyNote}
