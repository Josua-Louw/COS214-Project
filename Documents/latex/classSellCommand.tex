\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Concrete command for selling a plant in the \doxylink{classPlant}{Plant} Nursery Simulator. 

Implements the \doxylink{classCommand}{Command} Pattern to encapsulate the task of processing a plant sale. Assigned to \doxylink{classSalesManager}{Sales\+Manager} via \doxylink{classNurseryHub}{Nursery\+Hub}, it interacts with a \doxylink{classPlant}{Plant} and \doxylink{classOrder}{Order} to complete a customer transaction, updating inventory and supporting the Builder/\+Decorator Patterns for order customization.
