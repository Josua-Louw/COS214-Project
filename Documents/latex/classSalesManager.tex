\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Concrete staff class for handling customer sales in the \doxylink{classPlant}{Plant} Nursery Simulator. 

Inherits from \doxylink{classStaff}{Staff} to manage sales tasks, implementing the \doxylink{classCommand}{Command} Pattern (FR5) via receive\+Command to process \doxylink{classSellCommand}{Sell\+Command} tasks. Participates in the Chain of Responsibility Pattern (FR6) by receiving delegated tasks via next\+Staff. Interacts with \doxylink{classCustomer}{Customer} and \doxylink{classNurseryHub}{Nursery\+Hub} (Mediator, FR7) to facilitate sales and order processing.
