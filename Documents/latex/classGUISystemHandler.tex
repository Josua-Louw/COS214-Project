\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Manages greenhouse operations and interfaces with the GUI. 

This class extends \doxylink{classSystemHandler}{System\+Handler} to implement specific functionalities such as adding plants, registering staff members, processing customer orders, and providing summaries of the greenhouse state. It utilizes various care strategies to manage plant care effectively.
